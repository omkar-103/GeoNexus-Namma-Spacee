Official objective: Build an end-to-end intelligent spatial engine that converts standard smartphone captures of indoor environments into interactive 3D digital twins. The system should support remote first-person walkthroughs, spatial Point of Interest (POI) discovery and obstacle-aware indoor path planning. The longer-term vision includes on-site phone-camera navigation.

Round 1 focuses on:
1. A Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for capturing an indoor space.
2. 3D model reconstruction from captured data.
3. A zero-install desktop/web viewer.
4. Smooth first-person free roaming using standard controls such as WASD and mouse.
5. Free roaming must work even when active navigation is not running.

POI search and obstacle-aware desktop pathfinding are the focus of Round 2. AR navigation is an advanced bonus. Do not describe future or mock functionality as completed Round 1 functionality.
